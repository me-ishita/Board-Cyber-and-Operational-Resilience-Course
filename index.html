Board Cyber and Operational Resilience

Strengthen governance, regulatory alignment and crisis response in the cyber space.

Overview Course details Learning journey

Overview

This programme is built for senior and board-level leaders who need to reshape how their organisation approaches digital risk. With the PRA and FCA extending supervisory oversight — and government guidance now urging firms to strengthen defences against AI-enabled cyber threats — accountability sits squarely with the boardroom. The challenge is enabling confident oversight of resilience and incident response, even where technical depth at board level is limited.

Moving from compliance to capability, the course addresses what cyber resilience now demands: board-level ownership, integration with operational resilience, and clear regulatory alignment. You will leave with a stronger, more holistic view of risk — one that supports decisive action and sustained organisational resilience.

Across two focused, practical days, you will explore:

  • Characteristics of a resilient leader under crisis
  • The ecosystem of cyber threats and resilience
  • Incident response and crisis handling
  • Governance structures and controls
  • Third-party risk oversight

Learning Journey

Expert instructors combine with real-world exercises and applied practice to strengthen leadership capability — so you can lead with confidence through cyber disruption.

1

Regulatory Expectations

Understanding PRA and FCA requirements for board-level cyber governance, Bank of England oversight frameworks, and Board accountability under SM&CR and operational resilience rules.

2

Topical Threats & Response Strategies

The current cyber threat landscape facing UK financial services, building operational resilience and business continuity frameworks, and incident management protocols for board-level decision-making.

3

Cyber in an Interconnected Threat Landscape

Cyber risk sits within a wider, interconnected ecosystem. A single incident can create cascading impacts across operations, third parties, and critical infrastructure such as energy and supply chains.

4

Are You Resilient in a Crisis

In a crisis, outcomes depend on the resilience of those in charge. Boards must make critical decisions under pressure with limited information, while maintaining clarity and control.

5

Peer Learning & Network Strength

Leveraging shared experience and real-world scenarios to strengthen Board effectiveness, and building the professional networks that enable faster insight and collective resilience.
